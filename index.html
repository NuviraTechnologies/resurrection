✕ LEFT FOR DEAD — PAYPAL'S NATIVE HANDLING

What happens today when a member's payment fails.
  • DAY 0A payment fails. The member is told nothing.
  • DAY 5Silent retry #1. Fails again. Still no word to the member.
  • DAY 10Silent retry #2. Fails. That's the whole policy.
  • DAY 10+Subscription suspended. Membership over. The business finds out later — or never.
  • RESULTMember gone · $0 recovered · Nobody was ever told why
